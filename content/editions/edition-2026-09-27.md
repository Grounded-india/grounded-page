### Report

The train, a Diesel Electric Multiple Unit, operates with two 1,400 HP Driving Power Cars that were converted at the Sabarmati Integrated Coaching Depot to run on both fuels (google_news_india). Among the initiatives, the minister laid the foundation stone for the indigenous Kavach automatic train protection system across 404 km of the Ahmedabad division, covering the 157 km Ahmedabad-Palanpur section and the 247 km Ahmedabad-Samakhiyali section at an estimated cost of Rs 411 crore (google_news_india). Kavach is designed to prevent collisions, guard against overspeeding and mitigate risks linked to human error (google_news_india).

An automatic block signalling system was also introduced across 122.57 km of sections between Sabarmati-Kalol-Bhandu Motidau and Sabarmati-Sanand-Jakhwada at a cost of around Rs 118.83 crore, aimed at maintaining safe distances between trains and increasing route capacity (google_news_india). Shah inaugurated a WAG-12 locomotive maintenance shed at Sabarmati, built at an estimated Rs 700 crore, which will service 300 high-horsepower electric locomotives of 12,000 HP capacity designed for heavy and long freight trains (google_news_india). The Sabarmati-Sarkhej railway line doubling project, estimated at Rs 313.27 crore, will cover a 21-km stretch including stations at Sabarmati, Gandhigram, Vastrapur and Sarkhej, with bridges and road underpasses, targeting completion by March 2028 to improve connectivity toward the Sarkhej-Dholera region (google_news_india).

The projects collectively target improved railway safety, higher operational capacity and the adoption of alternative-fuel technology in rail transport (google_news_india). The dual-fuel DEMU represents the first deployment of LNG propulsion on the Indian rail network, following earlier exploration of hydrogen-based traction (google_news_india).
